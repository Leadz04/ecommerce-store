3. **Etsy Tab:** Click on "Etsy" tab in sidebar (5 seconds)
4. **Show Features:** 
   - Point out "Etsy Shop Connection" section (10 seconds)
   - Show "Sync Controls" section (10 seconds)
   - Show "Product Sync to Etsy" section (10 seconds)
   - Show "Export Products for Etsy" CSV export (15 seconds)

## Screenshot Tips

1. **Use full-screen captures** when possible
2. **Highlight important features** with annotations if needed
3. **Ensure text is readable** - zoom in if necessary
4. **Include URL bar** in browser screenshots to show it's your domain
5. **Name files clearly:** `etsy-integration-admin-dashboard.png`, `etsy-csv-export.png`, etc.
6. **Take multiple angles** if a feature requires scrolling
